What is matar paneer?

Matar paneer (also spelled mutter paneer) is a North Indian curry of paneer cubes and green peas in a spiced onion-tomato gravy. The Punjabi style is richer, with well-browned onions, a little cream and kasuri methi, and it's a regular at family dinners and dhabas.

About my recipe

Matar paneer is one of those dishes that feels like a restaurant dinner but uses things most of us already have: a block of paneer, a bag of frozen peas and a couple of tomatoes.

The Punjabi-style gravy here is built on browned onions, tomatoes and a little cream. For kids, take out a portion before adding the chilli powder, and cut the paneer into small cubes.

How to make it

  1. 1

    Soak the paneer cubes in hot water for 10 minutes so they stay soft. If you like, pan-fry them first in a little oil until lightly golden, then soak.

  2. 2

    Heat oil in a pan. Add cumin and bay leaf. When the cumin sizzles, add the onion and cook for 8 to 10 minutes until golden brown.

  3. 3

    Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute. Add the tomato purée, turmeric, coriander powder, chilli powder and salt.

  4. 4

    Cook for 6 to 8 minutes, stirring, until the masala thickens and the oil starts to separate.

  5. 5

    Add the peas and hot water. Simmer for 5 minutes until the peas are tender.

  6. 6

    Drain the paneer and add it with the cream. Simmer for 2 to 3 minutes. Stir in garam masala, kasuri methi and coriander. Serve with roti, naan or rice.

Expert tips

  • Soak the paneer in hot water before adding it. It keeps it soft instead of rubbery.
  • Brown the onions properly. That's where the Punjabi-style depth comes from.
  • Cook the masala until the oil separates before adding water, or the gravy tastes raw.
  • Add the paneer last and simmer only briefly.

FAQs

What is the difference between matar paneer and paneer butter masala?

Matar paneer has peas and a lighter onion-tomato gravy. Paneer butter masala has no peas and a richer, buttery tomato gravy.

Can I make matar paneer without onion and garlic?

Yes. Skip them, add a pinch of hing with the cumin and use a little extra tomato and cashew paste for body.

How do I make it less spicy for kids?

Leave out the chilli powder and add a spoon of cream or yogurt to their portion.

Can I freeze matar paneer?

The gravy freezes well. Paneer can go slightly crumbly after freezing, so add fresh paneer when reheating if you can.

Matar paneer, Punjabi style

Soft paneer and green peas in a creamy onion-tomato gravy. A Punjabi-style matar paneer recipe that's ready in 35 minutes and mild enough for kids.

Prep
10 min
Cook
25 min
Total
35 min
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 200 g paneer, cut into cubes
  • 1 cup green peas (frozen is fine)
  • 2 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 bay leaf (optional)
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 medium tomatoes, puréed
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1½ tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
  • 1 tsp salt (adjust)
  • 1 cup hot water
  • 2 tbsp cream or 1 tbsp cashew paste
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp kasuri methi, crushed
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander

Method

  1. Soak the paneer cubes in hot water for 10 minutes so they stay soft. If you like, pan-fry them first in a little oil until lightly golden, then soak.
  2. Heat oil in a pan. Add cumin and bay leaf. When the cumin sizzles, add the onion and cook for 8 to 10 minutes until golden brown.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute. Add the tomato purée, turmeric, coriander powder, chilli powder and salt.
  4. Cook for 6 to 8 minutes, stirring, until the masala thickens and the oil starts to separate.
  5. Add the peas and hot water. Simmer for 5 minutes until the peas are tender.
  6. Drain the paneer and add it with the cream. Simmer for 2 to 3 minutes. Stir in garam masala, kasuri methi and coriander. Serve with roti, naan or rice.
