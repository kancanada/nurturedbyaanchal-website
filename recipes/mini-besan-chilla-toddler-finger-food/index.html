What is besan chilla?

Besan chilla is a savoury pancake made from chickpea flour batter, often called a vegetarian omelette. Made small, they're perfect toddler finger food and a quick breakfast for the rest of us.

About my recipe

Besan chillas are a breakfast staple in many North Indian homes. Make them small and they become the perfect finger food for toddlers learning to feed themselves.

Grate in whatever vegetables you have: carrot, zucchini and spinach all work.

How to make it

  1. 1

    Whisk the besan with water to a smooth pouring batter, like pancake batter.

  2. 2

    Stir in the vegetables, turmeric, ajwain and salt. Rest for 5 minutes.

  3. 3

    Heat a non-stick pan and brush with a little oil.

  4. 4

    Pour small spoonfuls of batter (about 3 inches across) and cook for 2 minutes until the edges lift.

  5. 5

    Flip and cook another 1–2 minutes until golden. Serve warm with yogurt.

Expert tips

  • Rest the batter for 10 minutes. The chillas turn out softer and hold together better.
  • Squeeze grated zucchini dry before adding it, or the batter gets watery.
  • Cook on medium heat so the inside cooks before the outside browns.
  • Make them small, about 3 inches, so they're easy to flip and easy for little hands.

FAQs

Can I make the batter ahead?

Yes. It keeps a day in the fridge. Stir and add a splash of water before cooking.

Why do my chillas break?

The batter is too thin or the pan isn't hot enough. Add a spoon of besan and wait for the pan to heat.

Can I freeze them?

Yes. Freeze cooked chillas with parchment between them and reheat in a pan.

Mini besan chillas (toddler finger food)

Small savoury chickpea-flour pancakes with grated veg. Soft, easy to hold, and great for little hands.

Prep
5 min
Cook
15 min
Total
20 min
Serves
3

Ingredients

  • 1 cup besan (chickpea flour)
  • ¾ cup water (approx.)
  • ¼ cup grated carrot
  • ¼ cup finely chopped spinach
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped onion (optional)
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • ¼ tsp ajwain (carom seeds)
  • Pinch of salt
  • Oil or ghee for the pan

Method

  1. Whisk the besan with water to a smooth pouring batter, like pancake batter.
  2. Stir in the vegetables, turmeric, ajwain and salt. Rest for 5 minutes.
  3. Heat a non-stick pan and brush with a little oil.
  4. Pour small spoonfuls of batter (about 3 inches across) and cook for 2 minutes until the edges lift.
  5. Flip and cook another 1–2 minutes until golden. Serve warm with yogurt.
