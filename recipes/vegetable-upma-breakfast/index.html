What is upma?

Upma is a South Indian breakfast of roasted semolina (rava/sooji) cooked with a tempering of mustard seeds, curry leaves and vegetables. It's warm, filling and ready in about 20 minutes.

About my recipe

Busy mornings need breakfasts that don't take a lot of thought. Upma is that for me: one pan, pantry ingredients, and it keeps you full until lunch.

Roasting the semolina first is the step that stops it going lumpy.

Ingredients & substitutes

You'll need 11 ingredients, most of them pantry staples. The full amounts are in the recipe card. If you're missing something, here's what I swap in:

Semolina (rava)Broken wheat (dalia) or vermicelli. Both need a little more water.
Urad and chana dal in the temperingLeave them out, or use a handful of cashews for crunch.
Curry leavesSkip them, though they really make the dish.
Mixed vegetablesFrozen peas, carrots and beans.

How to make it

  1. 1

    Dry-roast the semolina in a pan on medium heat for 4–5 minutes until it smells nutty. Set aside.

  2. 2

    Heat oil, add mustard seeds and let them pop. Add urad dal and curry leaves.

  3. 3

    Add onion, ginger and vegetables and cook for 3 minutes.

  4. 4

    Pour in the hot water and salt and bring to a boil.

  5. 5

    Lower the heat and add the semolina slowly, stirring constantly, so no lumps form.

  6. 6

    Cover and cook on low for 2 minutes. Fluff up, squeeze in lemon and add coriander.

Expert tips

  • Roast the rava until it smells nutty but stays pale. It stops the upma from going lumpy.
  • Have the water boiling before you add the rava, and pour the rava in slowly while stirring.
  • Use about 2½ cups of water to 1 cup of rava for soft, fluffy upma.
  • Finish with a spoon of ghee and a squeeze of lemon.

FAQs

Why is my upma lumpy?

The rava went in too fast or the water wasn't boiling. Add it in a slow stream while stirring.

Can I roast rava in advance?

Yes. Roast a big batch, cool it and store it in a jar for up to a month.

Is upma good for toddlers?

Yes. Make it a little softer with extra water and leave out the green chilli.

10-minute vegetable upma

A warm, filling South Indian breakfast from semolina and veggies. Faster than toast once you've made it twice.

Prep
5 min
Cook
12 min
Total
17 min
Serves
3

Ingredients

  • 1 cup fine semolina (sooji / rava)
  • 1 tbsp oil or ghee
  • ½ tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 tsp urad dal (optional)
  • 8–10 curry leaves
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • ½ cup mixed vegetables (peas, carrot, beans)
  • 2½ cups hot water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Lemon and coriander to finish

Method

  1. Dry-roast the semolina in a pan on medium heat for 4–5 minutes until it smells nutty. Set aside.
  2. Heat oil, add mustard seeds and let them pop. Add urad dal and curry leaves.
  3. Add onion, ginger and vegetables and cook for 3 minutes.
  4. Pour in the hot water and salt and bring to a boil.
  5. Lower the heat and add the semolina slowly, stirring constantly, so no lumps form.
  6. Cover and cook on low for 2 minutes. Fluff up, squeeze in lemon and add coriander.
