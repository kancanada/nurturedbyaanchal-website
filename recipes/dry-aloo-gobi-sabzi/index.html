Dry aloo gobi sabzi

Potato and cauliflower with simple spices. Goes with roti, dal-rice, or straight into a lunchbox.

Weeknight dinners · 35 min · serves 4 · Jump to recipe

Aloo gobi is one of those sabzis that works with everything. The trick is to let the cauliflower get a little colour before covering the pan, so it doesn't go mushy.

Cut the pieces small and it doubles as finger food for toddlers.

A note from me

Dry aloo gobi sabzi

Prep
10 min
Cook
25 min
Total
35 min
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 medium cauliflower, cut into small florets
  • 2 medium potatoes, cubed
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 green chilli, slit (optional)
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • Coriander to finish

Method

  1. Heat oil in a wide pan. Add cumin, green chilli and ginger.
  2. Add potatoes and cook for 5 minutes, stirring.
  3. Add cauliflower, turmeric, coriander powder, chilli powder and salt. Toss well.
  4. Cook uncovered on medium-high for 5 minutes for a little colour, then cover and cook on low for 12–15 minutes, stirring once or twice, until tender.
  5. Sprinkle garam masala and coriander. Serve hot.
