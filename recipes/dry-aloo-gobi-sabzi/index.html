What is aloo gobi?

Aloo gobi is a dry stir-fry of potatoes and cauliflower with turmeric, cumin and a little ginger. It's one of the most common sabzis in Indian homes, and it packs well into lunchboxes.

About my recipe

Aloo gobi is one of those sabzis that works with everything. The trick is to let the cauliflower get a little colour before covering the pan, so it doesn't go mushy.

Cut the pieces small and it doubles as finger food for toddlers.

How to make it

  1. 1

    Heat oil in a wide pan. Add cumin, green chilli and ginger.

  2. 2

    Add potatoes and cook for 5 minutes, stirring.

  3. 3

    Add cauliflower, turmeric, coriander powder, chilli powder and salt. Toss well.

  4. 4

    Cook uncovered on medium-high for 5 minutes for a little colour, then cover and cook on low for 12–15 minutes, stirring once or twice, until tender.

  5. 5

    Sprinkle garam masala and coriander. Serve hot.

Expert tips

  • Cut the potatoes slightly smaller than the cauliflower so they finish at the same time.
  • Dry the cauliflower well after washing, or it will steam instead of roast.
  • Cover for the first part of cooking, then uncover so the edges brown.
  • Don't stir too often. Let it sit and catch a little colour.

FAQs

Why is my aloo gobi mushy?

Too much stirring or too much moisture. Dry the florets and stir only every few minutes.

Can I bake it?

Yes. Toss everything with oil and spices and roast at 220°C (425°F) for 25–30 minutes, flipping once.

Is it good for lunchboxes?

Yes. It holds well at room temperature and is easy to wrap in a roti.

Dry aloo gobi sabzi

Potato and cauliflower with simple spices. Goes with roti, dal-rice, or straight into a lunchbox.

Prep
10 min
Cook
25 min
Total
35 min
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 medium cauliflower, cut into small florets
  • 2 medium potatoes, cubed
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 green chilli, slit (optional)
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • Coriander to finish

Method

  1. Heat oil in a wide pan. Add cumin, green chilli and ginger.
  2. Add potatoes and cook for 5 minutes, stirring.
  3. Add cauliflower, turmeric, coriander powder, chilli powder and salt. Toss well.
  4. Cook uncovered on medium-high for 5 minutes for a little colour, then cover and cook on low for 12–15 minutes, stirring once or twice, until tender.
  5. Sprinkle garam masala and coriander. Serve hot.
