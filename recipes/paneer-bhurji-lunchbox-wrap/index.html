Paneer bhurji lunchbox wraps

Scrambled spiced paneer rolled into a roti. Ready in 15 minutes and travels well in a lunchbox.

Lunchbox · 15 min · serves 3 · Jump to recipe

This is a protein-packed lunch that comes together faster than you can find the car keys. Leftover rotis work perfectly.

For toddlers, skip the green chilli and cut the wrap into small pinwheels.

Paneer bhurji lunchbox wraps

Prep
5 min
Cook
10 min
Total
15 min
Serves
3

Ingredients

  • 200 g paneer, crumbled
  • 1 tbsp oil or butter
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 small tomato, finely chopped
  • ¼ cup finely chopped capsicum (bell pepper)
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp chilli powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp garam masala
  • Coriander, chopped
  • 3 rotis or tortillas
  • Green chutney or ketchup (optional)

Method

  1. Heat oil in a pan. Add cumin, then onion, and cook for 3 minutes.
  2. Add tomato and capsicum and cook until soft.
  3. Add turmeric, chilli powder and salt, then the crumbled paneer. Stir for 2–3 minutes. Don't overcook or the paneer gets rubbery.
  4. Finish with garam masala and coriander.
  5. Warm the rotis, spread a little chutney, add the bhurji and roll up tightly. Wrap in foil for the lunchbox.
