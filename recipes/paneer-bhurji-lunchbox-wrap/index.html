What is paneer bhurji?

Paneer bhurji is crumbled paneer cooked with onion, tomato and mild spices, a vegetarian cousin of scrambled eggs. Rolled in a roti, it's the fastest high-protein lunch I make.

About my recipe

This is a protein-packed lunch that comes together faster than you can find the car keys. Leftover rotis work perfectly.

For toddlers, skip the green chilli and cut the wrap into small pinwheels.

Expert tips

  • Crumble the paneer by hand, not in a processor, so it keeps some texture.
  • Add the paneer last and cook it for just 2–3 minutes, or it turns rubbery.
  • Let the filling cool before wrapping a lunchbox roll so it doesn't go soggy.
  • A spoon of yogurt mixed with mint makes a quick sauce for the wrap.

FAQs

Can I make the filling ahead?

Yes. It keeps 2 days in the fridge. Warm it gently before wrapping.

Is homemade paneer better?

It's softer, but store-bought works well. Soak it in hot water for 10 minutes first to soften it.

Is it toddler-friendly?

Yes, with the green chilli left out. Serve the bhurji on its own or in small pieces of roti.

Paneer bhurji lunchbox wraps

Scrambled spiced paneer rolled into a roti. Ready in 15 minutes and travels well in a lunchbox.

Prep
5 min
Cook
10 min
Total
15 min
Serves
3

Ingredients

  • 200 g paneer, crumbled
  • 1 tbsp oil or butter
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 small tomato, finely chopped
  • ¼ cup finely chopped capsicum (bell pepper)
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp chilli powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp garam masala
  • Coriander, chopped
  • 3 rotis or tortillas
  • Green chutney or ketchup (optional)

Method

  1. Heat oil in a pan. Add cumin, then onion, and cook for 3 minutes.
  2. Add tomato and capsicum and cook until soft.
  3. Add turmeric, chilli powder and salt, then the crumbled paneer. Stir for 2–3 minutes. Don't overcook or the paneer gets rubbery.
  4. Finish with garam masala and coriander.
  5. Warm the rotis, spread a little chutney, add the bhurji and roll up tightly. Wrap in foil for the lunchbox.
