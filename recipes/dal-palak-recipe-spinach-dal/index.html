What is dal palak?

Dal palak is lentils cooked with spinach and finished with a tempering of ghee, cumin and garlic. It's made across North India, usually with toor or moong dal, and served with rice or roti as an everyday meal.

About my recipe

Dal palak is everyday dal with a big handful of spinach stirred in. It's a quiet, comforting dinner, and it's one of the easiest ways I know to get greens onto the table without anyone making a fuss.

For little ones, chop the spinach finely or blend part of the dal so the leaves disappear. Keep the dried chillies for the grown-ups' tadka.

How to make it

  1. 1

    Pressure cook the dal with the water and turmeric for 3 to 4 whistles (or simmer for 30 to 35 minutes) until soft. Whisk it lightly.

  2. 2

    Heat ghee in a pot. Add cumin and hing, then half the garlic, and fry until the garlic is light golden.

  3. 3

    Add the onion, ginger and green chilli and cook for 3 to 4 minutes. Add the tomato and cook until soft.

  4. 4

    Add the spinach and cook for 2 to 3 minutes until it wilts.

  5. 5

    Pour in the cooked dal and salt. Add hot water if it is too thick and simmer for 5 minutes.

  6. 6

    For the tadka, heat a little ghee in a small pan, fry the rest of the garlic until golden, turn off the heat, add the red chillies and chilli powder and pour over the dal. Finish with lemon.

Expert tips

  • Add the spinach near the end and don't overcook it, so it keeps its colour.
  • Wash spinach well in a bowl of water. Grit sinks to the bottom.
  • A second garlic tadka at the end makes the dal taste much richer.
  • Dal thickens as it sits. Loosen leftovers with hot water.

FAQs

Can I use baby spinach?

Yes. It cooks even faster, so add it in the last minute or two.

Is dal palak good for kids?

It's mild and soft. Chop the spinach finely or blend part of the dal, and leave out the chillies for little ones.

Can I make it in an Instant Pot?

Yes. Pressure cook the dal for 8 minutes with natural release, then make the spinach masala and tadka on Sauté.

How long does it keep?

Up to 3 days in the fridge, or 2 months in the freezer.

Dal palak (spinach dal)

Toor dal cooked with fresh spinach and finished with a garlic and cumin tadka. An easy dal palak recipe for rice or roti, and a simple way to add greens.

Prep
10 min
Cook
25 min
Total
35 min
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup toor dal (or moong dal), rinsed
  • 3 cups water
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 3 packed cups spinach, washed and chopped
  • 1 tsp salt (adjust)
  • 1 tbsp ghee or oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • Pinch of hing (asafoetida)
  • 5 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 1 green chilli, slit (optional)
  • ½ tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
  • 1 to 2 dried red chillies (optional)
  • Squeeze of lemon

Method

  1. Pressure cook the dal with the water and turmeric for 3 to 4 whistles (or simmer for 30 to 35 minutes) until soft. Whisk it lightly.
  2. Heat ghee in a pot. Add cumin and hing, then half the garlic, and fry until the garlic is light golden.
  3. Add the onion, ginger and green chilli and cook for 3 to 4 minutes. Add the tomato and cook until soft.
  4. Add the spinach and cook for 2 to 3 minutes until it wilts.
  5. Pour in the cooked dal and salt. Add hot water if it is too thick and simmer for 5 minutes.
  6. For the tadka, heat a little ghee in a small pan, fry the rest of the garlic until golden, turn off the heat, add the red chillies and chilli powder and pour over the dal. Finish with lemon.
