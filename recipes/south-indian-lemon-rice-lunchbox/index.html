What is lemon rice?

Lemon rice (chitranna in Kannada, elumichai sadam in Tamil) is cooked rice tossed with a tempering of mustard seeds, dals, curry leaves, chillies and peanuts, coloured with turmeric and finished with fresh lemon juice. It's a South Indian travel and lunchbox staple because it tastes good without reheating.

About my recipe

Lemon rice is my answer to the bowl of leftover rice sitting in the fridge. Ten minutes, one pan, and it's a lunch that doesn't need a microwave at work.

Cold, day-old rice works better than freshly cooked rice here, because the grains stay separate instead of turning mushy.

Ingredients & substitutes

You'll need 14 ingredients, most of them pantry staples. The full amounts are in the recipe card. If you're missing something, here's what I swap in:

Day-old riceFreshly cooked rice spread on a plate to cool for 20 minutes.
PeanutsCashews, or skip for a nut-free school lunch and add roasted chana dal for crunch.
Sesame oilAny neutral oil or ghee.
Chana and urad dalSkip them; the rice will still taste good, just with less crunch.

How to make it

  1. 1

    Fluff the cooled rice with a fork so the grains separate. Sprinkle the salt over it.

  2. 2

    Heat oil in a wide pan. Add peanuts and fry for 2 minutes until golden, then add mustard seeds and let them pop.

  3. 3

    Add chana dal and urad dal and stir until light golden. Add red chillies, curry leaves, hing and ginger and stir for 20 seconds.

  4. 4

    Turn the heat to low and add the turmeric, then immediately add the rice.

  5. 5

    Toss gently for 2 to 3 minutes until the rice is evenly yellow and heated through.

  6. 6

    Turn off the heat, then add the lemon juice and coriander and toss again. Taste and add more salt or lemon if needed.

Expert tips

  • Cool the rice completely before mixing so the grains don't break.
  • Keep the heat low once the turmeric goes in so it doesn't burn.
  • Add lemon juice after turning off the heat for the freshest flavour.
  • Make it a full meal with a side of yogurt, cucumber or leftover sabzi.

FAQs

Can I use basmati rice?

Yes. Any cooked rice works, though shorter South Indian rice like sona masoori is traditional.

How long does it keep?

Up to 2 days in the fridge. For lunchboxes, pack it in the morning and eat within a few hours.

Is lemon rice spicy?

Only mildly. Leave out the red chillies for kids.

Can I make it without onion and garlic?

Yes. This recipe has neither.

South Indian lemon rice (lunchbox favourite)

Leftover rice tossed with a mustard, curry leaf and peanut tempering and plenty of lemon. Tastes great at room temperature, so it's perfect for lunchboxes.

Prep
5 min
Cook
10 min
Total
15 min
Serves
3

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked rice, cooled (day-old is best)
  • 2 tbsp oil (sesame or any neutral oil)
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 tsp chana dal (optional)
  • 1 tsp urad dal (optional)
  • 2 tbsp raw peanuts or cashews
  • 1 to 2 dried red chillies, broken
  • 10 curry leaves
  • Pinch of hing (asafoetida)
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp salt (adjust)
  • 3 tbsp lemon juice (about 1 large lemon)
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander

Method

  1. Fluff the cooled rice with a fork so the grains separate. Sprinkle the salt over it.
  2. Heat oil in a wide pan. Add peanuts and fry for 2 minutes until golden, then add mustard seeds and let them pop.
  3. Add chana dal and urad dal and stir until light golden. Add red chillies, curry leaves, hing and ginger and stir for 20 seconds.
  4. Turn the heat to low and add the turmeric, then immediately add the rice.
  5. Toss gently for 2 to 3 minutes until the rice is evenly yellow and heated through.
  6. Turn off the heat, then add the lemon juice and coriander and toss again. Taste and add more salt or lemon if needed.
