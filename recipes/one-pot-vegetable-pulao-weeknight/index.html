What is vegetable pulao?

Vegetable pulao (pulav) is basmati rice cooked with whole spices, onion and mixed vegetables so the grains stay separate and lightly fragrant. It's milder and quicker than biryani, and it's a weeknight staple in a lot of North Indian homes.

About my recipe

Vegetable pulao is what I make when I want rice that feels a little special without much extra work. Everything goes into one pot, the whole spices do most of the flavouring, and frozen vegetables are completely fine.

Serve it with plain yogurt or raita for the kids, and with dal or a quick curry if you want a bigger meal. Cubes of paneer stirred in at the end make it more filling.

Ingredients & substitutes

You'll need 16 ingredients, most of them pantry staples. The full amounts are in the recipe card. If you're missing something, here's what I swap in:

Basmati riceAny long-grain rice. Adjust the water to the packet instructions.
Whole spices½ tsp extra garam masala if you don't have cloves, cardamom or cinnamon.
Fresh vegetablesA frozen mixed vegetable bag works well, straight from the freezer.
GheeAny neutral oil.

How to make it

  1. 1

    Rinse the rice 3 or 4 times until the water runs mostly clear. Soak it in fresh water for 15 to 20 minutes, then drain.

  2. 2

    Heat ghee in a heavy pot. Add cumin, bay leaf, cloves, cardamom and cinnamon and stir for 30 seconds until fragrant.

  3. 3

    Add the onion and cook for 5 to 6 minutes until light golden. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chilli and stir for 1 minute.

  4. 4

    Add the vegetables, garam masala and salt and stir for 2 minutes.

  5. 5

    Add the drained rice and stir gently for 1 minute so the grains are coated. Pour in the water and bring to a boil.

  6. 6

    Cover tightly, turn the heat to low and cook for 12 to 15 minutes until the water is absorbed and the rice is tender. Turn off the heat and rest, covered, for 5 minutes.

  7. 7

    Fluff with a fork, fold in the paneer if using, and finish with coriander and mint. Remove the whole spices before serving to little ones.

Expert tips

  • Soaking the rice for 15 to 20 minutes helps the grains cook evenly and stay long.
  • Use about 1½ cups of water per cup of soaked basmati for separate, fluffy grains.
  • Don't stir the rice once the lid goes on. Stirring breaks the grains.
  • Rest it covered for 5 minutes before fluffing. The steam finishes the cooking.

FAQs

Can I make pulao in an Instant Pot?

Yes. Sauté the spices, onion and vegetables, add soaked rice and 1¼ cups water per cup of rice, and pressure cook for 5 minutes with a 10-minute natural release.

Why is my pulao mushy?

Too much water, or the rice was stirred while cooking. Measure the water and leave the lid on.

What do I serve with vegetable pulao?

Raita, plain yogurt, dal, chana masala or a simple cucumber salad.

Is pulao good for kids?

It's mild and easy to eat. Remove the whole spices and leave out the green chilli for little ones.

One-pot vegetable pulao (30 minutes)

Basmati rice cooked with whole spices, carrots, peas and beans in one pot. Mild enough for kids, and lovely with raita or a bowl of dal.

Prep
10 min
Cook
20 min
Total
30 min
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups basmati rice
  • 2 tbsp ghee or oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 4 cloves
  • 3 green cardamom pods
  • 1 small cinnamon stick
  • 1 medium onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 green chilli, slit (optional)
  • 1½ cups mixed vegetables (carrot, peas, green beans, corn)
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • 1¼ tsp salt (adjust)
  • 2¼ cups water
  • 100 g paneer, cubed (optional)
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander and mint

Method

  1. Rinse the rice 3 or 4 times until the water runs mostly clear. Soak it in fresh water for 15 to 20 minutes, then drain.
  2. Heat ghee in a heavy pot. Add cumin, bay leaf, cloves, cardamom and cinnamon and stir for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Add the onion and cook for 5 to 6 minutes until light golden. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chilli and stir for 1 minute.
  4. Add the vegetables, garam masala and salt and stir for 2 minutes.
  5. Add the drained rice and stir gently for 1 minute so the grains are coated. Pour in the water and bring to a boil.
  6. Cover tightly, turn the heat to low and cook for 12 to 15 minutes until the water is absorbed and the rice is tender. Turn off the heat and rest, covered, for 5 minutes.
  7. Fluff with a fork, fold in the paneer if using, and finish with coriander and mint. Remove the whole spices before serving to little ones.
