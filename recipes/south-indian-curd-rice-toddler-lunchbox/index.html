What is curd rice?

Curd rice (thayir sadam in Tamil, mosaranna in Kannada, perugu annam in Telugu) is cooked rice mixed with yogurt and topped with a tempering of mustard seeds, curry leaves and chillies. It's usually eaten at the end of a South Indian meal, on hot days or packed for travel, because it tastes good without reheating.

About my recipe

Curd rice is the comfort food of so many South Indian homes, and it's one of the easiest meals I know to make from leftover rice. It's mild, it's soft, and it doesn't need reheating, which makes it a good lunchbox option.

For a toddler, I mash the rice a little more and keep the tempering very simple. The grown-up bowls get green chilli, ginger and a spoon of pickle on the side.

How to make it

  1. 1

    If the rice is cold from the fridge, sprinkle 2 tbsp water over it and warm it for a minute so it softens. Mash it lightly with the back of a spoon.

  2. 2

    Let the rice cool to room temperature, then mix in the warm milk. This keeps it creamy and stops it turning sour later.

  3. 3

    Add the yogurt and salt and mix well. It should be soft and loose. Add a little more milk or yogurt if it looks dry.

  4. 4

    Heat oil or ghee in a small pan. Add mustard seeds and let them pop, then add urad dal and stir until light golden.

  5. 5

    Add curry leaves, hing, ginger, green chilli and red chilli. Stir for 20 seconds and turn off the heat.

  6. 6

    Pour the tempering over the rice and mix. Stir in the carrot or cucumber, top with pomegranate and coriander, and serve at room temperature or slightly chilled.

Expert tips

  • Mix the yogurt in once the rice has cooled, or the yogurt can split and turn grainy.
  • Adding a little milk keeps curd rice from turning sour in a lunchbox.
  • It thickens as it sits, so make it looser than you want it to be when you eat it.
  • For toddlers, mash the rice well, skip the chillies and cut any toppings small.

FAQs

Can I pack curd rice for lunch?

Yes. Pack it in an insulated container with an ice pack and eat it within a few hours. Adding milk helps it stay mild.

Why is my curd rice sour?

The yogurt was already sour, or it sat too long in a warm place. Use fresh plain yogurt and add a little milk.

Can I use leftover rice?

Yes, it's ideal. Warm it with a splash of water so it softens, then let it cool before adding yogurt.

Is curd rice OK for toddlers?

It's soft and mild. Leave out the chillies and whole seeds for little ones, and check with your doctor about your own child's diet.

South Indian curd rice (thayir sadam)

Soft rice mixed with yogurt and finished with a mustard seed and curry leaf tempering. Cooling, mild and ready in 15 minutes, for toddlers and grown-ups alike.

Prep
5 min
Cook
10 min
Total
15 min
Serves
3

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice (leftover is fine)
  • ¼ cup milk, warm
  • 1 cup plain yogurt (curd), not sour
  • ½ tsp salt (adjust)
  • 1 tbsp oil or ghee
  • ½ tsp mustard seeds
  • ½ tsp urad dal (optional)
  • 8 to 10 curry leaves
  • Pinch of hing (asafoetida)
  • ½ tsp grated ginger
  • 1 green chilli, finely chopped (optional)
  • 1 dried red chilli (optional)
  • 2 tbsp grated carrot or cucumber (optional)
  • 2 tbsp pomegranate seeds (optional)
  • 1 tbsp chopped coriander

Method

  1. If the rice is cold from the fridge, sprinkle 2 tbsp water over it and warm it for a minute so it softens. Mash it lightly with the back of a spoon.
  2. Let the rice cool to room temperature, then mix in the warm milk. This keeps it creamy and stops it turning sour later.
  3. Add the yogurt and salt and mix well. It should be soft and loose. Add a little more milk or yogurt if it looks dry.
  4. Heat oil or ghee in a small pan. Add mustard seeds and let them pop, then add urad dal and stir until light golden.
  5. Add curry leaves, hing, ginger, green chilli and red chilli. Stir for 20 seconds and turn off the heat.
  6. Pour the tempering over the rice and mix. Stir in the carrot or cucumber, top with pomegranate and coriander, and serve at room temperature or slightly chilled.
