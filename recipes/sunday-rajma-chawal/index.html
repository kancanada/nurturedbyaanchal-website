What is rajma chawal?

Rajma chawal is red kidney beans simmered in a thick onion-tomato gravy, served over rice. It's the Sunday lunch of many North Indian homes, the kind of meal that makes the whole house smell good.

About my recipe

Rajma chawal is a Sunday tradition in a lot of Punjabi homes. It takes a little longer, but most of that time is hands-off simmering while you get on with the day.

Short on time? Canned kidney beans work. Just reduce the simmering to 15–20 minutes.

How to make it

  1. 1

    If using dried rajma, drain the soaked beans and pressure cook with fresh water for 6–7 whistles until completely soft. Kidney beans must be fully cooked.

  2. 2

    Heat oil, add bay leaf and cumin, then onions. Cook for 10–12 minutes until deep golden.

  3. 3

    Add ginger-garlic paste for 1 minute, then tomato purée and dry spices except garam masala. Cook until the oil separates.

  4. 4

    Add the cooked beans with their liquid (or canned beans and 2 cups water) and salt. Simmer for 20 minutes, mashing a few beans to thicken.

  5. 5

    Stir in garam masala and coriander. Serve over hot rice.

Expert tips

  • Soak the rajma for at least 8 hours. Old or unsoaked beans never get creamy.
  • Cook the beans until they mash easily between your fingers before adding them to the gravy.
  • Mash a ladle of beans into the gravy to thicken it.
  • Simmer at least 15 minutes after combining so the flavours come together.

FAQs

Why is my rajma still hard?

The beans may be old, or weren't soaked long enough. Keep pressure cooking in 5-minute rounds until soft.

Is undercooked rajma safe?

No. Raw or undercooked kidney beans can make you sick. Always cook them until fully soft.

Can I freeze rajma?

Yes. It freezes well for up to 3 months.

Sunday rajma chawal

Slow-simmered kidney beans in an onion-tomato gravy. The weekend comfort meal, with enough left for Monday.

Prep
15 min
Cook
45 min
Total
60 min
Serves
5

Ingredients

  • 1 cup dried rajma (kidney beans), soaked overnight, or 2 cans, drained
  • 2 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 3 tomatoes, puréed
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • 3 cups water
  • Coriander to serve
  • Steamed basmati rice to serve

Method

  1. If using dried rajma, drain the soaked beans and pressure cook with fresh water for 6–7 whistles until completely soft. Kidney beans must be fully cooked.
  2. Heat oil, add bay leaf and cumin, then onions. Cook for 10–12 minutes until deep golden.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste for 1 minute, then tomato purée and dry spices except garam masala. Cook until the oil separates.
  4. Add the cooked beans with their liquid (or canned beans and 2 cups water) and salt. Simmer for 20 minutes, mashing a few beans to thicken.
  5. Stir in garam masala and coriander. Serve over hot rice.
