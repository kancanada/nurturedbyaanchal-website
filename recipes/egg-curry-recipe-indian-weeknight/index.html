What is egg curry?

Indian egg curry (anda curry) is hard-boiled eggs simmered in a spiced onion-tomato gravy. It's made all over India, from Punjabi dhaba-style versions to South Indian ones with coconut, and it's one of the quickest curries to make from pantry staples.

About my recipe

Egg curry is the dinner for nights when the fridge looks empty but there's a carton of eggs. The masala is the same onion-tomato base used in a lot of North Indian curries, and the eggs only need boiling.

For kids, take out a portion of the masala before adding the chilli, and chop their egg into small pieces. Grown-ups get the slit green chillies and a squeeze of lemon.

Ingredients & substitutes

You'll need 16 ingredients, most of them pantry staples. The full amounts are in the recipe card. If you're missing something, here's what I swap in:

Fresh tomatoes1 cup canned crushed tomatoes or 2 tbsp tomato paste with ½ cup water.
Kashmiri chilli powderPaprika for colour without much heat.
Kasuri methiLeave it out, or add a little extra coriander.
OilGhee or butter for a richer gravy.

How to make it

  1. 1

    Put the eggs in a pot, cover with cold water and bring to a boil. Boil for 10 minutes, then move them to cold water. Peel and make 2 or 3 shallow slits on each egg so the masala soaks in.

  2. 2

    Heat oil in a pan. Add cumin and the bay leaf and let the cumin sizzle.

  3. 3

    Add the onions and cook for 8 to 10 minutes on medium heat until golden. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chilli and stir for 1 minute.

  4. 4

    Add the tomato purée, turmeric, coriander powder, chilli powder and salt. Cook for 5 to 6 minutes, stirring, until the oil starts to separate.

  5. 5

    Pour in the hot water and simmer for 3 minutes. Add the eggs and simmer for 5 more minutes, spooning the gravy over them.

  6. 6

    Stir in the garam masala and crushed kasuri methi. Finish with coriander and serve with rice or roti.

Expert tips

  • Start the eggs in cold water and cool them quickly after boiling. They peel more easily.
  • Make small slits on the eggs so the gravy gets inside.
  • Brown the onions properly. It's what gives the curry its depth.
  • Lightly fry the boiled eggs with a pinch of turmeric and chilli before adding them, if you like a firmer, golden outside.

FAQs

Can I make egg curry without onion?

Yes. Use extra tomato and a pinch of hing in the tempering. The gravy will be lighter.

How do I make egg curry less spicy for kids?

Leave out the green chilli and chilli powder, and stir a spoon of yogurt or cream into their portion.

What do I serve with egg curry?

Steamed rice, jeera rice, roti, paratha or plain naan.

How long does egg curry keep?

Up to 2 days in the fridge. Reheat gently. Boiled eggs do not freeze well, so it is best not to freeze it.

Easy Indian egg curry (anda curry)

Boiled eggs simmered in a simple onion-tomato masala. A 35-minute Indian egg curry recipe that goes with rice or roti, with a milder version for kids.

Prep
10 min
Cook
25 min
Total
35 min
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 6 eggs
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 bay leaf (optional)
  • 2 medium onions, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 to 2 green chillies, slit (optional)
  • 2 medium tomatoes, puréed
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1½ tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp salt (adjust)
  • 1 cup hot water
  • ½ tsp kasuri methi (optional)
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander

Method

  1. Put the eggs in a pot, cover with cold water and bring to a boil. Boil for 10 minutes, then move them to cold water. Peel and make 2 or 3 shallow slits on each egg so the masala soaks in.
  2. Heat oil in a pan. Add cumin and the bay leaf and let the cumin sizzle.
  3. Add the onions and cook for 8 to 10 minutes on medium heat until golden. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chilli and stir for 1 minute.
  4. Add the tomato purée, turmeric, coriander powder, chilli powder and salt. Cook for 5 to 6 minutes, stirring, until the oil starts to separate.
  5. Pour in the hot water and simmer for 3 minutes. Add the eggs and simmer for 5 more minutes, spooning the gravy over them.
  6. Stir in the garam masala and crushed kasuri methi. Finish with coriander and serve with rice or roti.
