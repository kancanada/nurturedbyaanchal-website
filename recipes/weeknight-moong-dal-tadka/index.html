30-minute moong dal tadka

The dal I make when it's 6pm, everyone is hungry and I have nothing planned. Yellow moong cooks fast and needs no soaking.

Weeknight dinners · 30 min · serves 4 · Jump to recipe

If there's one recipe every working mom should have memorised, it's a fast dal. Yellow moong is the hero here: it cooks in about 20 minutes on the stove (or 3 whistles in a pressure cooker), and it's gentle enough for little ones too.

I take a small bowl out for my toddler before the tadka goes in, then add the chilli for the grown-ups.

30-minute moong dal tadka

Prep
5 min
Cook
25 min
Total
30 min
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 cup yellow moong dal, rinsed well
  • 3½ cups water
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp salt (adjust)
  • 1 tbsp ghee or oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • Pinch of hing (asafoetida)
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1–2 dried red chillies (optional)
  • ½ tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
  • Handful of chopped coriander
  • Squeeze of lemon

Method

  1. Add the rinsed dal, water, turmeric and salt to a pot. Bring to a boil, skim the foam, then simmer partly covered for 20–25 minutes until soft (or pressure cook for 3 whistles).
  2. Whisk the cooked dal lightly so it turns creamy. Add a splash of hot water if it's too thick.
  3. For the tadka, heat ghee in a small pan. Add cumin and hing, and let the cumin sizzle.
  4. Add garlic and fry until just golden, then the onion until soft. Add tomato and cook for 3–4 minutes until it breaks down.
  5. Turn off the heat, stir in the red chillies and chilli powder, and pour the tadka over the dal.
  6. Finish with coriander and lemon. Serve with rice or roti.
