What is vegetable khichdi?

Khichdi is rice and lentils cooked together until soft and porridge-like, with whatever vegetables you have. It's the comfort food most Indian kids grow up on, and the easiest way I know to get dal, rice and veg into a toddler in one bowl.

About my recipe

Khichdi is the original comfort food in so many Indian homes, and it's one of the easiest ways to get vegetables into a toddler. Everything goes in one pot, so there's only one pot to wash.

Keep the spices mild for little ones and add pickle or a spicier tadka on the side for adults.

  4. 4

  5. 5

  6. 6

Expert tips

  • Cook it softer than you'd like for yourself. Toddlers prefer it mushy, and you can thicken the adult portion on the stove.
  • Keep salt very light for little ones and add more to your own bowl.
  • A spoon of ghee on top right before serving makes a big difference to the taste.
  • Freeze small portions in an ice-cube tray for quick toddler meals.

FAQs

What age can babies eat khichdi?

Many families start with a smooth, unsalted khichdi once a baby is on solids. Check with your paediatrician about your own child.

Can I add spinach?

Yes. Stir in a handful of chopped spinach for the last 5 minutes of cooking.

Why is my khichdi too thick?

It firms up as it cools. Stir in hot water a little at a time until it's the texture you want.

One-pot vegetable khichdi (toddler-approved)

Rice, dal and whatever vegetables are in the fridge, all in one pot. Soft enough for toddlers, cosy enough for the whole family.

Prep
10 min
Cook
25 min
Total
35 min
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • ½ cup rice (any short or basmati)
  • ½ cup yellow moong dal
  • 1 tbsp ghee
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • Pinch of hing
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 1½ cups chopped vegetables (carrot, peas, beans, pumpkin, spinach)
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp salt (less for toddlers)
  • 4–5 cups water

Method

  1. Rinse the rice and dal together until the water runs clear. Soak for 10 minutes if you have time.
  2. Heat ghee in a pressure cooker or heavy pot. Add cumin, hing and ginger and stir for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the vegetables and stir for 2 minutes.
  4. Add the drained rice and dal, turmeric, salt and water.
  5. Pressure cook for 4 whistles, or simmer covered for 25–30 minutes, stirring now and then, until very soft.
  6. Mash lightly with the back of a spoon. Add hot water for a looser texture. Top with a little ghee before serving.
