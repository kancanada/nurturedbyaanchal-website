What is kanda poha?

Kanda poha is flattened rice cooked with onions (kanda in Marathi), mustard seeds, curry leaves, turmeric and peanuts, finished with lemon and coriander. It's a classic breakfast in Maharashtra and across much of India, served with a cup of chai.

About my recipe

Poha is the breakfast I reach for when I want something warm but don't have time for anything that needs a pressure cooker. The flattened rice only needs a quick rinse, and the whole thing is done in one pan.

For my toddler I skip the green chilli and keep the peanuts out of his bowl, then add extra lemon and sev to mine.

Ingredients & substitutes

You'll need 15 ingredients, most of them pantry staples. The full amounts are in the recipe card. If you're missing something, here's what I swap in:

Thick pohaMedium poha works too. Rinse it for less time, about 10 seconds. Avoid thin poha, which turns mushy.
PeanutsCashews, or leave them out for a nut-free lunchbox.
Green peasDiced potato (cook it before the onion), grated carrot or corn.
Lemon juiceLime juice, or a pinch of amchur (dry mango powder).

How to make it

  1. 1

    Put the poha in a strainer and rinse under running water for 15 to 20 seconds, tossing with your fingers, until just soft. Drain well and let it sit for 5 minutes.

  2. 2

    Sprinkle the turmeric, salt and sugar over the damp poha and toss gently so every flake is coated.

  3. 3

    Heat oil in a wide pan. Add the peanuts and fry for 2 minutes until they crackle and darken slightly. Lift them out and set aside.

  4. 4

    In the same oil, add mustard seeds. When they pop, add cumin, curry leaves and green chilli.

  5. 5

    Add the onion and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until soft and translucent. Add the peas and cook for 1 minute.

  6. 6

    Add the seasoned poha and the peanuts. Toss gently on low heat for 2 to 3 minutes, then cover for 1 minute to steam.

  7. 7

    Turn off the heat, squeeze in the lemon and add coriander. Serve warm with sev or coconut on top.

Expert tips

  • Use thick poha. It holds its shape after rinsing; thin poha dissolves.
  • Rinse, don't soak. Poha only needs a quick rinse and a few minutes of rest to soften.
  • Season the poha with turmeric and salt before it goes in the pan so the colour is even and you don't over-stir.
  • Add the lemon off the heat. Cooking it makes the poha taste bitter.

FAQs

Why is my poha dry or hard?

It wasn't rinsed long enough. Sprinkle a little water over it in the pan, cover and steam for 2 minutes.

Why is my poha mushy?

It was soaked too long or the poha was thin. Next time rinse for just 15 seconds and drain right away.

Can I pack it for lunch?

Yes. It's good at room temperature. Pack the sev separately so it stays crunchy.

Is poha OK for toddlers?

It's soft and easy to eat. Leave out whole peanuts and chilli for little ones, and check with your doctor about your own child's diet.

15-minute kanda poha

Flattened rice with onion, peanuts, turmeric and lemon. A light Maharashtrian breakfast that's soft enough for toddlers and fast enough for a workday.

Prep
5 min
Cook
10 min
Total
15 min
Serves
3

Ingredients

  • 2 cups thick poha (flattened rice)
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • ½ tsp mustard seeds
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • 8 to 10 curry leaves
  • 2 tbsp raw peanuts
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 1 green chilli, finely chopped (optional)
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • ¾ tsp salt (adjust)
  • 1 tsp sugar (optional)
  • ¼ cup green peas (frozen is fine)
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander
  • Sev or grated coconut to serve (optional)

Method

  1. Put the poha in a strainer and rinse under running water for 15 to 20 seconds, tossing with your fingers, until just soft. Drain well and let it sit for 5 minutes.
  2. Sprinkle the turmeric, salt and sugar over the damp poha and toss gently so every flake is coated.
  3. Heat oil in a wide pan. Add the peanuts and fry for 2 minutes until they crackle and darken slightly. Lift them out and set aside.
  4. In the same oil, add mustard seeds. When they pop, add cumin, curry leaves and green chilli.
  5. Add the onion and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until soft and translucent. Add the peas and cook for 1 minute.
  6. Add the seasoned poha and the peanuts. Toss gently on low heat for 2 to 3 minutes, then cover for 1 minute to steam.
  7. Turn off the heat, squeeze in the lemon and add coriander. Serve warm with sev or coconut on top.
