What is chana masala?

Chana masala is chickpeas simmered in a tangy onion-tomato gravy spiced with garam masala and amchur. It's a Punjabi classic, and with canned chickpeas it becomes a 40-minute weeknight dinner.

About my recipe

Chana masala is my favourite kind of recipe for a busy week: make a big pot on Sunday and you've got lunches sorted. Using canned chickpeas cuts the time right down.

Pack leftovers with rice or a roti for an easy work lunch.

    Add onion and cook for 8–10 minutes until golden. This is where the flavour comes from, so don't rush it.

Expert tips

  • Brown the onions well. It takes 8–10 minutes, and that's where the deep flavour comes from.
  • Mash a few chickpeas against the side of the pot to thicken the gravy naturally.
  • Let it rest 10 minutes before serving. The chickpeas soak up the masala.
  • It tastes even better the next day, so it's a great one to make ahead.

FAQs

Can I use dried chickpeas?

Yes. Soak them overnight, then pressure cook for 35 minutes (or 15 in an Instant Pot) before adding to the gravy.

How do I make it less spicy for kids?

Leave out the green chilli and chilli powder. Add a spoon of yogurt to the kids' bowls.

What do I serve it with?

Rice, roti, bhature or naan, with sliced onion and lemon on the side.

Easy chana masala (stovetop or Instant Pot)

A make-ahead favourite that tastes even better the next day. Canned chickpeas make it a true weeknight dinner.

Prep
10 min
Cook
30 min
Total
40 min
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (540 ml each) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tomatoes, puréed (or 1 cup canned crushed tomatoes)
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
  • 1½ tsp chana masala or garam masala
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 tsp amchur (dry mango powder) or lemon juice
  • Coriander and sliced onion to serve

Method

  1. Heat oil in a pot (or Instant Pot on Sauté). Add cumin and let it sizzle.
  2. Add onion and cook for 8–10 minutes until golden. This is where the flavour comes from, so don't rush it.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute, then the tomato purée and dry spices. Cook until the oil separates, about 5 minutes.
  4. Add chickpeas, salt and water. Simmer for 15 minutes, mashing a few chickpeas to thicken the gravy (or pressure cook for 5 minutes).
  5. Stir in amchur or lemon. Serve topped with coriander and onion.
