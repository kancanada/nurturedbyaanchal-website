Hi, I'm Aanchal

A working mom in Vancouver, cooking for a family of three and trying to look after myself too.

I work full time in home insurance. My husband Anuj and I have a two-year-old son, and most of our evenings are about getting a good dinner on the table without it taking all night.

I also have PCOS. I'm not a doctor or a nutritionist, so I don't tell anyone what to eat. I share the South Asian meals and routines that work for me and my family, and I'm honest about what doesn't.

Nurtured by Aanchal started on Instagram, where I talk about postpartum fitness, women's health, marriage and family, and South Asian life in Canada. This site is where the recipes and routines live in full.
