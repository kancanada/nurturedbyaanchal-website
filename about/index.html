My story

A working mom in Vancouver, trying to look after everyone, including herself.

Becoming a mom changed everything, including how much of me was left at the end of the day.

I'm Aanchal. I work full time in home insurance, and my husband Anuj and I are raising our two-year-old son in Vancouver, in a home full of South Asian food, family and opinions.

After my son was born, I wanted my health and my time back. I started training again around my workday, and I started sharing it on Instagram as Nurtured by Aanchal.

Today I talk about postpartum and mom fitness, women's health, marriage and relationships, South Asian family life, travelling as a family and staying motivated when the to-do list never ends.

I'm not an expert or a doctor. I share what works for me and my family, and I'm honest about what doesn't.
