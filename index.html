Becoming a mom changed everything, including how much of me was left at the end of the day.

I work full time in home insurance. My husband Anuj and I are raising our two-year-old son in Vancouver, in a home full of South Asian food, family and culture.

Somewhere in the middle of all that, I decided my health and my time mattered too. So I started training again, and I started sharing it, the good days and the messy ones.

Now I share what actually works for us: workouts that fit around a workday, family meals, marriage, and the conversations South Asian families don't always have out loud.
