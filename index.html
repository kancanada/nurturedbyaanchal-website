First, there was the bump.

A full-time job, a growing belly, and a lot of advice from everyone who had an opinion. I didn't know yet how much my life, and I, were about to change.

02

Then he arrived, and everything changed.

Including how much of me was left at the end of the day. Postpartum is an identity shift nobody prepares you for. I decided my health and my time mattered too.

03

Now: the juggle.

Home insurance by day. A two-year-old by evening. Workouts squeezed in somewhere. A husband, Anuj, who's in it with me. It isn't perfect, and I don't pretend it is.

04

So I started talking.

About mom guilt, marriage, money, log kya kahenge, and the feminism nobody explains properly. It turns out millions of women were thinking the same thing.
